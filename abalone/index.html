How to play

Shove 6 of your opponent's marbles off the board to win.

Select: tap one of your marbles, then tap adjacent same‑colour marbles to build a straight line of up to 3 (teal).

Move: tap a gold cell to slide there — inline or sideways.

Push: aim your line end‑on at a shorter opponent line (2‑beats‑1, 3‑beats‑1 or ‑2) and tap the red front marble. Equal lines can't be pushed.
