How to play

The board fills as you play. Clear marbles before it does.

Select: tap one of your marbles — it lifts, and every square it can reach lights up gold.

Move: tap a lit square. The marble can only travel through empty squares (no jumping).

Clear: form a line of 5 or more of the same colour — horizontal, vertical or diagonal — and it vanishes for points. Longer lines score more.

Each turn three new marbles drop in (see Next) — unless your move just cleared a line, which grants a free move.
